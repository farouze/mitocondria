## 1. Journal of Microscopy

The official scope includes microscopy advances and applications, and its Original Article category is more appropriate for this multi-experiment study than a short protocol. Its guidelines request a single-column, double-spaced, 12-point manuscript, line numbering, a separate lay description of at most 400 words, and full corresponding-author details. Numbered superscript references replace the current IEEE bracket style. Use six keywords to satisfy both the six- and seven-keyword instructions appearing on the page. Expand “3D” in the title to follow its no-abbreviation title instruction. [Author guidelines](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/13652818/homepage/forauthors.html).

**My recommendation:** first specialist target after a clean reproduction and preferably the paired offset experiment. The main editorial risk is that the work could be seen as a dataset-specific audit unless its transferable measurement lessons and methodological evidence are clear. The prepared lay description and practitioner points are in `Submission_Notes.md`.

## 2. Microscopy and Microanalysis

The journal covers microscopy techniques and microstructural analysis in biology and materials science, with originality and significance among its criteria. Submission adaptation includes a maximum 200-word abstract without abbreviations, double spacing, author–year references with complete author lists, and figure alt text. Its data policy encourages accessible data/code and requires appropriate dataset citations. [Journal scope](https://academic.oup.com/mam/pages/about) and [author instructions](https://academic.oup.com/mam/pages/general-instructions).

**My recommendation:** second specialist target, especially if the controlled generation experiment produces a well-characterized methodological result. Verify the optional APC separately; the official pricing page exceeded the web reader's size limit and blocked the direct retrieval attempt. The no-charge standard route was explicitly verified, so an unknown optional APC should not be misread as an unavoidable publication cost.

## 3. PLOS ONE

PLOS ONE evaluates technical rigor and whether conclusions follow from the evidence rather than judging perceived importance. That makes it a sensible alternative for a careful measurement study with openly reported failures and limited generalization. [Reviewer criteria](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/reviewer-guidelines).

The abstract maximum is 300 words; the journal does not impose a general manuscript-length or figure-count limit. LaTeX submissions are initially supplied as PDFs. Adapt figure/table placement and separate submission files, and complete data, funding and competing-interest information. [Submission guidelines](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submission-guidelines) and [submission files](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submit-now). Data supporting the findings must be publicly available by publication, subject to the policy's exceptions. [Data policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability).

**My recommendation:** preferred broad-scope alternative once reproduction is documented and the evidence package is ready for reviewers. Fee assistance, if needed, must be requested at submission rather than after acceptance. [Fee assistance rules](https://plos.org/fees/).

## 4. Scientific Reports

Its scope encompasses original research across natural sciences, medicine and engineering. [Scope](https://www.nature.com/srep/about). Its guidance sets a 200-word abstract, a title of at most 20 words, and recommends a main text no longer than 4,500 words excluding the abstract, methods, references and figure legends; confirm mandatory limits through its linked checklist. It advises an article length of about 11 typeset pages. The publisher's eventual page count is not the same as the working IEEE PDF's count. [Submission guidelines](https://www.nature.com/srep/author-instructions/submission-guidelines).

**My recommendation:** broad-scope alternative after the same scientific and reproducibility revisions. A concise main narrative and a separate exploratory supplement would help. The optional generic short abstract in `Submission_Notes.md` supplies a starting point, not a claim of final venue compliance.

## Submission order and decision

Start with **Journal of Microscopy** if a specialist microscopy readership is the priority. Consider **Microscopy and Microanalysis** if the additional intervention strengthens the methods contribution. Use **PLOS ONE** as the leading broad-scope alternative; **Scientific Reports** is another reasonable option. Submit sequentially, not to multiple journals at once.

Do not choose on impact factor alone, and do not equate the 76/100 audit rating with an acceptance percentage. Editorial fit and reviewer judgment remain uncertain. I would not make a high-novelty algorithmic or biological-discovery journal the first target on the current evidence.
