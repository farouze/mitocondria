# Manuscript audit and corrections — revision 8

Audit date: September 25, 2026. Input: revision 7, the latest local revised manuscript, together with its retained numerical evidence and the original references. Original attachments and synced sources were not changed.

## Outcome

All 33 original bibliography entries correspond to real works/resources, including the author's real but unpublished local Homeostatic Invariance draft. No fabricated reference was identified. The unpublished draft lacked a public URL, so it was removed from the bibliography while its proposed framework was retained and explicitly distinguished from a tested biological result. A direct, verified mesh-fusion software citation was added. The final bibliography has 33 entries, all cited in the text or tables and all with URLs.

**Reference existence is verified; universal link accessibility is not.** Several publishers and PubMed served bot challenges or blocked automated requests. A HTTP 200 challenge page was not treated as accessible article content. DOI metadata and indexed abstracts corroborate those works, but some full-text access still requires a normal browser or institutional subscription. Link behavior can change after this audit. The rows below and the CSV distinguish accessible records from access-limited endpoints. Machine-readable verification links are provided where available; these are metadata, not substitutes for reading the complete paper.

Final bibliography numbers with access limits at the displayed endpoint: **1, 2, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 20, 21, 23, 33**. These are not claims that the references are broken or fictitious.

## Corrections made

- Corrected Faitg et al. from a review to a method combining electron microscopy and cytochrome c oxidase labeling, in both the introduction and Table I.
- Removed unsupported adoption language (“widely used”) for the mixed set of established and recently introduced tools.
- Removed redundant author-by-author citation repetition in Related Work; separated tool descriptions into focused sentences; reduced clustered dataset citations in Tables I–II. No adjacent citation-command groups remain. Citations in a comparison table remain attached to the relevant resource.
- Added the official NeurIPS link and the bootstrap book's publisher link; supplied accessible author/institution or conference links for Faitg, Occupancy Networks, marching cubes, and generalized winding numbers. Canonical DOI fields are preserved in the bibliography source.
- Corrected the MitoGraph citation to a book chapter. Added CVF-version pages to Occupancy Networks; recorded its alternate IEEE pagination below.
- Verified EMPIAR's 2022 release date against its official API; its 2021 DOI deposit is not a contradictory publication claim.
- Added the exact public mesh-fusion source-file reference for numerical fusion settings. The historical generating commit and causal offset-on/off experiment remain unresolved.
- Attached the bootstrap citation to the method rather than implying that the source supplied this paper's seeds and sample counts.
- Softened the surface-area sentence to supported estimator dependence, avoiding a universal claim about voxel-face overestimation.
- Kept Homeostatic Invariance explicitly proposed. The data do not measure respiration, ATP, membrane potential, preserved function, or a biological transition threshold.

## Numerical and factual boundaries

This pass changes literature descriptions, citation placement, bibliography metadata, and links. It does not change the numerical results or run the notebooks again. Retained values were compared with the packaged reproduction summaries: 2,720 development objects, 2,728 second-batch objects; second-batch corrected MdAPE 0.6635948%, RMSE 1.742827%, and 2,512/2,728 coverage (92.0821%). The earlier boundary replay retains the 57 originally accepted fits and documents all 60 local fits. MitoEM includes 550 eligible objects and the earlier 9,350 phase/tie remeshings. These are within-resource/object-level evaluations, not independent-animal validation.

The 3DMSL article's full text explicitly reports 10,000 query points and 24 nm resampling. The retained 100,000-query claim is based on released files and build code; the manuscript properly identifies this discrepancy rather than changing the article's account. The entire simulated-imaging and point-cloud workflow was not independently rerun, so those retained results remain supported by supplied notebook records rather than new execution in this pass.

No audit can certify that a paper contains zero possible errors. This audit found and corrected the specific unsupported/misleading descriptions above. It does not establish the untested causal mechanism, prospective chronology, biological independence, historical code-version identity, or generalizability beyond the analyzed data.
